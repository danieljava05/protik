# Legal and trust documents (drafts)

> **These are drafts written by the engineering team, not legal advice.** They describe what the software actually does so
> that a Nigerian lawyer has accurate facts to work from. Nothing here has been reviewed by counsel, and the project must not
> be launched publicly, or accept real users' data, until it has been.

| Document | What it is | State |
|---|---|---|
| [PRIVACY-NOTICE.draft.md](PRIVACY-NOTICE.draft.md) | What personal data the software holds, why, for how long, and the user's rights | Draft: facts accurate to the code, legal text and bracketed items need counsel |
| [TERMS.draft.md](TERMS.draft.md) | Rules of use and what the service promises | Draft |
| [CHILD-SAFETY-PROCEDURE.md](CHILD-SAFETY-PROCEDURE.md) | How a child-safety report must be handled, step by step, and what the software can and cannot do | Draft: reporting duties and authorities left for counsel |

Questions only a lawyer can answer are marked **[COUNSEL]**. Decisions only the operator can make are marked **[OPERATOR]**.
They are collected at the end of each document.
