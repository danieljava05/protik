# Instructions for AI coding agents

These are standing rules, not suggestions.

1. Read [VISION.md](VISION.md) and [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)
   completely before writing code. CONTRIBUTING.md is binding.
2. Before any ticket: review the area (modules, schema, DESIGN.md),
   compare at least two designs, choose one, and record it as an ADR
   in `docs/adr/` using the template.
3. Challenge weak tickets or earlier decisions before building them.
4. Never claim something works unless you ran it. Report what was and
   was not verified, including failing tests.
5. One ticket at a time, finished to the Definition of Done.
6. No new dependencies, modules or features beyond the ticket without
   written justification.
