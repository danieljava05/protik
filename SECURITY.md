# Security Policy

ProTik is pre-alpha and not deployed. Please still report issues.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Do not open a public issue. Use GitHub's private vulnerability
reporting ("Security" tab -> "Report a vulnerability") on this
repository.

Include what you found, how to reproduce it, and the impact. We aim to
acknowledge reports within 7 days.

## Scope

Anything in this repository. Do not test against systems you do not own,
and do not access or retain other people's data.
